    <div>
      <div class="paper-title">
        How Fuel Subsidy Reductions Reshaped Fishing Activity:
        Evidence from China’s Policy Reform in the East China Sea
      </div>

      <div class="paper-authors">
        with
        <a href="https://www.efdinitiative.org/about-efd/people/renau-jorge-marco">
          Jorge Marco
        </a>
      </div>

      <div class="paper-status">
        R&amp;R — Environmental and Resource Economics
      </div>
    </div>

    <a href="https://jorgephd.github.io/jlmontero.github.io/assets/papers/Fuel_Subsidies_Fishing.pdf"
       target="_blank"
       class="paper-button">
      PDF
    </a>

  </div>

  <details class="paper-abstract">
    <summary>Abstract</summary>

    <p>
      This paper examines how fuel subsidy reductions affect fishing activity, exploiting the 2016 reform of China’s fuel subsidy program in the East China Sea as a quasi-natural experiment. The reform reduced average subsidy payments by 69% and introduced an asymmetric distribution across vessel sizes, with larger vessels receiving 15.8% more support than smaller ones.
    </p>

  </details>

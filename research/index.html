How Fuel Subsidy Reductions Reshaped Fishing Activity: Evidence from China’s Policy Reform in the East China Sea
with Jorge Marco
R&R — Environmental and Resource Economics
PDF
Abstract

This paper examines how fuel subsidy reductions affect fishing activity, exploiting the 2016 reform of China’s fuel subsidy program in the East China Sea as a quasi-natural experiment. The reform reduced average subsidy payments by 69% and introduced an asymmetric distribution across vessel sizes, with larger vessels receiving 15.8% more support than smaller ones.
